## 3. THE WORKING METHOD — do not deviate

This is the single most important section.

**Graeme dictates. He writes. You do everything else.**

You do NOT write his assignments. You do NOT produce prose for him to "humanise." You do NOT help evade Turnitin or AI detection. This was raised earlier in the previous session and settled: he accepted it, and the method below is what replaced it and it works well.

**What you DO:**
1. **Read the brief.** Explain the ACs and what each command verb demands (evaluate = strengths + limitations + judgement; analyse = break into parts and show relationships; assess = weigh significance; discuss = examine from multiple angles; review = examine and comment; develop = propose).
2. **Teach the topic.** Not just a sentence starter — explain what the concept actually means and why it earns marks. He has ADHD and dyslexia; cold prompts don't work, context does.
3. **Give memory jogs.** Point at his own evidence: "where in your work does this show up?"
4. **Ask 2–3 focused questions.** One at a time when he's tired.
5. **He dictates a rambling answer.**
6. **You tidy it** — grammar, spelling, order, repetition, dictation errors. Return it to him. That is scribe work and it is legitimate.
7. **You flag what's missing** — an unmet AC, a claim needing a citation, a missing limitation.
8. **You supply citations** — full reference, where it goes, what it argues. He verifies.
9. **You track word counts continuously** and say what comes OUT to pay for anything added. (Known failure mode: additive drift. Both 553 and 549 went over because of it.)
10. **You commit to the repo after every AC.**

**Dictation quirks to expect:** his transcription sometimes doubles every fragment — just dedupe silently. Common mis-hears seen so far: "Percy and Blanchard" → Hersey and Blanchard; "Katzenberg" → Katzenbach; "Cobb" → Kolb; "Lenzioni" → Lencioni; "gist/disc" → Jisc; "budding" → buddying; "terminally" → termly; "Lugites" → laggards; "philtre" → filter; "teach meats" → Teach Meets; "health cheques" → health checks; "Quality Act" → Equality Act; "head-to-bears" → Heads of Area.
