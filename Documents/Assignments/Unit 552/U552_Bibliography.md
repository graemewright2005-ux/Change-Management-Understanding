## Verification notes before submission

1. **Aguilar (1967)** is the conventional attribution for environmental scanning, from which PESTLE derives. PESTLE has no single agreed originator; the wording in Task 1 attributes the scanning concept rather than the acronym.
2. **Waterman, Peters and Phillips (1980)** is the primary source for the McKinsey 7S framework, not Peters and Waterman (1982), which popularised it in *In Search of Excellence*.
3. **Deming (1986)** also appears in Unit 553, where PDCA framed operational planning, and in Unit 551 as a project decision point. Here it frames iterative delivery through volunteers. Same source, three distinct applications, entirely fresh wording in each.
4. **Freeman (1984)** also appears in Unit 551. Used here for communication segmentation rather than stakeholder mapping.
5. **Kotter (1996) and Lewin (1947)** are evaluated against each other in AC1.3 and referenced again in AC3.1 where Kotter is explicitly rejected. Consistent treatment across both tasks.
6. **Ofsted (2025)** verified against the primary source on 30 August 2026. Correct title is *Further education and skills inspection toolkit*; "EIF" is the superseded predecessor.
7. **PSBAR 2018** requires WCAG 2.1 AA. WCAG 2.2 AA is applied as an internal college standard exceeding it. Stated correctly throughout.
8. **Dweck (2006)** added at Graeme's prompt during AC3.3 dictation. Verify before submission.
