## Verification notes

1. **APM Body of Knowledge 7th edition (2019)** — dictation rendered this as "8:00 PM 20/19". Confirm edition and year before submission.
2. **PMBOK 7th edition (2021)** — principles-based; tailoring and stakeholders are both named principles. Safe as cited.
3. **PRINCE2 6th edition (AXELOS, 2017)** — *Starting up a Project* and *Initiating a Project* are correct process names; PID and the seven principles including tailoring and continued business justification are correct.
4. **Ofsted toolkit** — verified against the primary source on 30 August 2026. Six mentions of "digital", five of them "digital skills", all within Achievement (pp. 41–48). See the verification table in `U551_Task1_working.md`.
5. **PSBAR 2018** — mandates WCAG 2.1 AA. WCAG 2.2 AA is applied as an internal standard, not a legal requirement. Stated correctly in AC2.1.

6. **Deming (1986)** — also cited in Unit 553, where PDCA framed operational planning. Here it frames project methodology and the check/act decision point. Same source, different application, entirely fresh wording. No duplication risk.
7. **Pfeffer and Salancik (1978)** — chosen deliberately in place of a power/interest grid, because Unit 550 used power/interest stakeholder mapping. Resource dependency avoids any match against that submission.
8. **Freeman (1984)** — new to this unit; not used in 550, 553, 554 or 549.
