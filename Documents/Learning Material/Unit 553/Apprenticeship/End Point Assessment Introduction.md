End Point Assessment Introduction
Welcome to your apprenticeship End Point Assessment (EPA) preparation unit. This unit is designed to help you prepare for your EPA for the Operations Manager occupational standard (ST0385 v1.4).

Now that you have completed the learning for the on-programme part of your apprenticeship, the next step is to prepare for the EPA – the final stage of your apprenticeship journey and the summative or final formal assessment required to achieve your apprenticeship and gain certification.

This unit works in a different way to those that you have worked through so far. It provides guidance about what to expect from your EPA and contains some tasks that will help you to begin to prepare for it.

The unit begins with an outline of the EPA process so that you know what to expect. This includes an explanation of the ‘gateway’ phase and information on the EPA process specific to the Operations Manager standard, including how its two assessment methods are used to assess learner competence. There is also a section to cover each aspect of the assessment methods to provide further guidance on best practice and help you to prepare.

All of the tasks are aligned with Skills England's apprenticeship standards. Below you will find some tips and advice to take on board before you get started.

Tips and Advice
You may find the following guidance helpful as you work through the unit.

Each task is broken down into four sections:

Task instructions – what you are expected to do to complete the task
Further guidance – advice and suggestions on how best to approach the task, including signposting to other elements of the course you might find useful
Resources – sources of knowledge to help you to complete the task and links to handouts for detailed background information and guidance
Task submission area – where you upload your completed work
Read every part of each task carefully, several times, before you begin. This is to ensure that you fully understand the requirements and have digested the advice and resources provided.

Use the tasks as opportunities to try out the methods used for the EPA process and as the basis of conversations with your tutor, employer or assessor. This will enrich the quality of the sources of information available to you, broaden your perspective and help to strengthen your EPA in practice.

Remember to look back at your learning from across your management apprenticeship course – we will link to specific sections and resources where they are referred to in the tasks.

Check the 'Resources' sections of each task for pointers on where to look for ideas and information, as well as support from colleagues. At the end of each task, there is also a references section, which provides links to external resources.

The task submission area indicates which format you should use to prepare your work (e.g. written report or presentation) and, where applicable, provides indicative lengths (e.g. sentence number or length requirements, word count, number of slides). Ensure that you are familiar with the upload system that follows each task.

When uploading your responses to the tasks, take care not to upload any documents that contain sensitive or confidential information (e.g. individuals’ names or internal data on your company’s financial performance).

Make sure you add your completed tasks to your learning record. If you have any questions about this, please refer to our Information for Apprenticeships page, the CPD Log page within each unit, or speak with your tutor.

Ensure that you fully familiarise yourself with the project calendar so that you know your deadlines in advance and can submit your responses to the tasks in good time.
