Section 2 - Preparing for the Written Project Report
 
   
Task 1: Structuring Your Written Project Report
Welcome to the first task of the End Point Assessment (EPA) preparation unit. This task aims to provide ideas on how to plan for the first of the two components of the ‘project with report and presentation’ assessment method.

As yet, you are unlikely to have reached the ‘gateway’ phase of the EPA process, however, this exercise will help you to consider how to plan to write your project report for the initiative that you delivered whilst working on your apprenticeship on-programme.

Remember that this exercise is for practice only – your actual EPA planning can begin once you have entered the gateway and have been instructed to do so by your tutor/assessor or End Point Assessment Organisation (EPAO).

 
   
Instructions
Using your experience from your workplace project – and the specialist knowledge you’ve gained about project management during your qualification – prepare a high-level ‘ideas plan’ document to help you think about how you will structure the written project report for your EPA.

Your ideas plan should focus on the project delivered while working on your apprenticeship on-programme that best highlights your project management skills and meets the competencies found in the knowledge, skills and behaviours (KSB) themes for this assessment method:

Project scope and planning
Project implementation
Evaluation and recommendations
You can find out more about the specific KSBs in each group from Skills England.

The ‘Task Upload’ area below includes further instructions and a template for completing the task itself.

Further guidance:

To guide your thinking, review the information in Section 1, as well as the additional resources below.

Resources:

To help you complete this task, we recommend:

Reviewing the Skills England ‘EPA plan’
Researching the EPA guidance criteria and grading descriptors for the EPAO chosen for your assessment. If CMI is your EPAO, you can access their apprenticeship information in their ‘L5 Operations manager apprenticeship handbook’
Making time with your tutor or assessor to understand more about the EPA process
Guide time: 1–2 hours.
