Section 2 - Preparing for the Written Project Report
 
   
The first End Point Assessment (EPA) method for the Operations Manager apprenticeship standard involves writing up a report about the project that you delivered on-programme, submitting it, and then presenting and answering questions about the project to an Independent End Point Assessor (IEPA) at your EPA meeting.

During your training period, you will have identified and delivered a suitable project relevant to your occupation and apprenticeship. The project should be a significant, defined piece of work with a real business application and benefit to your employer. It should also have sufficient scope to both meet the needs of the business as well as satisfy the project management criteria graded in this assessment.

Although the project will be delivered during your training, you will need to have started the EPA process – and have entered the EPA ‘gateway’ – before you begin writing up your project report. The assessment requires you to submit a 4,000-word written project report that meets the requirements of the project assessment criteria.

Once you have written and submitted your project report to the IEPA, you will also need to prepare a presentation on it, which you are required to present, and answer questions about it, as one of the assessment methods for your EPA.

The assessment themes you are looking to meet fall into three group themes:

Project scope and planning
Project implementation
Evaluation and recommendations
Once you have written and submitted your project report to the IEPA, you will also need to prepare a presentation on it, which you are required to present and answer questions based on, as one of the assessment methods for your EPA.

Where CMI is the End Point Assessment Organisation (EPAO), the presentation and questions will last at least 60 minutes – with the presentation typically lasting 20 minutes and the Q&A the remaining 40 minutes. The independent assessor will ask you at least six questions.

 
   
About the Project
For this assessment method, you will need to draw on the project that you completed whilst you were on-programme and during your apprenticeship training. Your project will have been signed off against the occupational standards and therefore should have provided you with sufficient scope to meet all the EPA criteria required for this assessment method.

According to Skills England, a typical project will take around six months to complete and may be delivered on a range of business topics, examples of which include:

Introduction of a new process, service or product to an operational area
Identification and implementation of a change plan to improve efficiency or make a direct saving (e.g. percentage decrease in direct costs or a reduction in headcount)
Implementation of a performance improvement plan for the operational area, which required significant collaboration with wider stakeholders
Evaluation of an internal communications project that involved communicating change to different stakeholders
You may have led the project yourself or worked as part of a team, including with internal colleagues, external stakeholders or technical experts. Therefore, it’s important to state exactly what role you played and which tasks you personally undertook as part of the project delivery.

Although you may have worked collaboratively on the project, you must complete your written project report and presentation unaided and ensure that you are reflective of your own role and contribution to it. Your employer must attest to the work you personally delivered when the written project report and any presentation materials are submitted.

You can find out more about the requirements for choosing suitable projects by looking at the ‘Project with report’ section of the Skills England ‘EPA plan’.

 
   
Structure
The written project report requires a word count of around 4,000 words – plus or minus 10% and excluding appendices, references and diagrams, which are not included in the total.

The report must clearly show how you met all of the criteria in the three assessment group themes required for the two components – the project report and presentation – of this assessment method:

Project scope and planning
Project implementation
Evaluation and recommendations
The written report has a required format and should include the following sections as a minimum:

Executive summary
Introduction
Scope and business need
Research and analysis outcomes
Project outcomes
Discussion of findings
Conclusions and recommendations
References
Appendices

Submission and Delivery
You must complete and submit the written project report – component one of the project and report assessment method – to the EPAO by the end of week 12 of the EPA period. Discuss with your tutor or assessor to find out whether there is a template that you should use. If CMI is your EPAO, there is an EPA portal for you to use to submit your written report and presentation.

You will also submit your presentation materials – component two of this assessment method – to the EPAO along with the report. See Section 3 for further details about preparing for your presentation.
