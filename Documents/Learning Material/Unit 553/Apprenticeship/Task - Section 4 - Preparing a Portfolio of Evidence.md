Section 4 - Preparing a Portfolio of Evidence
 
   
Task 3: Ideas for Creating Your Showcase EPA Portfolio
Welcome to the third task of the End Point Assessment (EPA) preparation unit. The aim of this task is to give you ideas for how to develop a ‘showcase’ portfolio that will underpin your EPA professional discussion.

 
   
Instructions
Using your on-programme portfolio of work-based evidence, complete the four steps to gathering evidence for one key group theme required for the professional discussion assessment method.

Consider the work you have completed during your apprenticeship because this can help you to decide what evidence to select and which pieces will best represent your work. You may also be able to identify how your work has improved as part of your learning journey and contrast earlier work with later versions to highlight this.

You should try to provide either a piece of work or combined evidence of multiple documents that will showcase the criteria and support your professional discussion.

The work must have been produced by you and collectively allow you to demonstrate all four key themes required for this assessment method:

Managing and leading a team
Organisational governance
Operational planning
Stakeholder relationships
The ‘Task Upload’ area below includes further instructions and a template for completing the task itself.

Further guidance:

To guide your thinking, you can find out more about what a portfolio of evidence should include in CMI’s ‘Start with the end in mind: Supporting your apprentices in building a showcase portfolio information.

Resources:

To help you complete this task, we recommend:

Reviewing the Skills England ‘EPA plan’
Researching the EPA guidance criteria and grading descriptors for the EPAO chosen for your assessment. If CMI is your EPAO, you can access their apprenticeship information in their ‘L5 Operations manager apprenticeship handbook’
Making time with your tutor or assessor to understand more about the EPA process
Guide time: 1–2 hours.
