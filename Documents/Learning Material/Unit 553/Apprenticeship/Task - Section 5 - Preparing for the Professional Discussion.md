Section 5 - Preparing for the Professional Discussion
Task 4: Ideas for Your EPA Professional Discussion
Welcome to the fourth task of the End Point Assessment (EPA) preparation unit. This task aims to provide ideas on how to plan for your EPA professional discussion, as supported by a portfolio of evidence.

As yet, you are unlikely to have reached the ‘gateway’ phase of the EPA process but you are likely to have begun compiling evidence for your EPA portfolio. This exercise will help you to consider what relevant work you want to showcase in your discussion and how it meets the EPA criteria for this assessment method.

Remember that this exercise is for practice only – your actual EPA planning can begin once you have entered the gateway and submitted your portfolio to your End Point Assessment Organisation (EPAO).

Instructions
Using your experience from the workplace – and the specialist knowledge you’ve gained about management and leadership during your qualification – prepare for a mock professional discussion, using your portfolio as supporting evidence.

The mock discussion should focus on the work that you have delivered while working on your apprenticeship programme (as contained in the portfolio of evidence) and relate to the four knowledge, skills and behaviours (KSB) themes required for this assessment:

Managing and leading a team
Organisational governance
Operational planning
Stakeholder relationships
Whilst preparing for your mock professional discussion, consider how you will structure the conversation to showcase and expand on the work you have submitted in your portfolio to demonstrate the required KSBs.

The ‘Task Upload’ area below includes further instructions and a template for completing the task itself.

Further guidance:

To guide your thinking, review the information in Section 5, as well as the additional resources below.

Resources:

To help you complete this part of the task, we recommend:

Reviewing the Skills England ‘EPA plan’
Researching the EPA guidance criteria and grading descriptors for the EPAO chosen for your assessment. If CMI is your EPAO, you can access their apprenticeship information in their ‘L5 Operations manager apprenticeship handbook’
Making time with your tutor or assessor to understand more about the EPA process
Guide time: 1–2 hours.
