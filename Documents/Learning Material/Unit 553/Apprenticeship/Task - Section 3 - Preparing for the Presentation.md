Section 3 - Preparing for the Presentation
 
   
Task 2: Ideas for Your EPA Presentation
Welcome to the second task of the End Point Assessment (EPA) preparation unit. This task aims to provide ideas on how to plan for your EPA presentation, based on the project work that you carried out whilst on-programme.

As yet, you are unlikely to have reached the ‘gateway’ phase of the EPA process, however, this exercise will help you to consider how you will present your workplace project and supplement the written project report to evidence your work in relation to the relevant project EPA criteria.

Remember that this exercise is for practice only – your actual EPA planning can begin once you have entered the gateway and have been registered with an End Point Assessment Organisation (EPAO).

 
   
Instructions
Using your experience from the workplace – and the specialist knowledge you’ve gained about management and leadership during your qualification – prepare a high-level ‘ideas plan’ document to help you prepare for how you would present a piece of work for EPA.

Consider the work you have completed during your apprenticeship because this can help you to formulate the conversations with your employer and tutor or assessor regarding work that could feature in your EPA presentation, depending on the topic allocated by the EPAO.

Your ideas plan should focus on a piece of work or project that you have completed or delivered while working on your apprenticeship programme. Ideally, you will have either led or significantly contributed to some elements of the deliverable.

The work must be relevant to your role and allow you to demonstrate the three key themes required for this assessment method:

Project scope and planning
Project implementation
Evaluations and recommendations
Whilst working through the ideas plan, consider how it might meet these three key themes.

Further guidance:

To guide your thinking, review the information in Section 3, as well as the additional resources below.

Resources:

To help you complete this task, we recommend:

Reviewing the Skills England ‘EPA plan’
Researching the EPA guidance criteria and grading descriptors for the EPAO chosen for your assessment. If CMI is your EPAO, you can access their apprenticeship information in their ‘L5 Operations manager apprenticeship handbook’
Making time with your tutor or assessor to understand more about the EPA process
Guide time: 1–2 hours.
