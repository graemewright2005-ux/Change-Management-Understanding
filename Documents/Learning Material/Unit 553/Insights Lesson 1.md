Corporate Social Responsibility: A Simple Guide
Business Dictionary defines corporate social responsibility (CSR) as “a company’s sense of responsibility towards the community and environment (both ecological and social) in which it operates”.

This online article answers some important questions – what is CSR? What are the benefits? Why is it important?

Jackson, R. (2024). Corporate social responsibility and environmental, social, and governance – a simple guide. The Giving Machine.


Access the article here: https://www.thegivingmachine.co.uk/blog/posts/corporate-social-responsibility-and-environmental-social-and-governance-a-simple-guide/

Health and Safety at Work Act 1974
The Health and Safety at Work Act 1974, covering occupational health and safety in the UK, is a primary piece of legislation that a manager needs to understand. This page provides an overview of the Act’s key aspects and some useful links.

Health and Safety Executive (N.D.). Health and Safety at Work etc Act 1974.


Access the overview here: https://www.hse.gov.uk/legislation/hswa.htm

Question: The ‘Health and safety law poster pocket card (PDF)’ linked to at the bottom of the page highlights ten things that employers must do and four things that employees must do. How many of these are acted upon by you and your organisation and are there any areas in which either you or your employer need to be more vigilant?

Question: The Act also covers the safety of non-employees and workers and includes a duty of care to members of the public, which may include the self-employed, suppliers and clients etc. Consider what actions you can take to protect the health and safety of any non-employees and workers in this way.
