Problem-solving Process
Problem-solving and decision-making are two essential skills required by an operational manager to be effective in their role.

This online article introduces a practical four-step approach to solve work-based problems.

Stahl, A. (2020) Four Steps to Solve Any Problems At Work, 10 September.


Access the article here: https://www.forbes.com/sites/ashleystahl/2020/09/10/4-steps-to-solve-any-problem-at-work/?sh=1cdec29c2220

Questions:

Stahl suggests that “we jump straight into action to put out a fire, rather than take the time to understand where the flame is starting from in the first place” – in other words, we don’t take enough time to understand a problem, before trying to solve it! Consider your approach to problem-solving – do you “jump straight into action”?
Identify a recent problem that you have solved. How did your approach compare to the one suggested? What are the similarities and differences?
Based on this reflection, how could you improve your current problem-solving approach?
Edward de Bono’s Six Thinking Hats
Edward de Bono developed a structured brainstorming technique based on six metaphorical ‘thinking’ hats.

This article provides insight into when and how to use this technique to analyse a situation, and decide on next steps.

Channell, M. (2021) The Six Thinking Hats: How to Improve Decision Making, with Examples, TSW Training Blog, 12 April 2021.


Access the article here: https://www.tsw.co.uk/blog/leadership-and-management/six-thinking-hats/

Questions:

How could you use the Six Thinking Hats® technique to analyse problems and make decisions in your operation?
The ‘blue’ hat is used by the leader of the brainstorming session to manage the process. How confident would you be in wearing the ‘blue’ hat?
How would your team respond to the idea of using metaphorical hats to support parallel thinking? If you think that this approach could detract from the brainstorming process, how could you reframe the concept to be more user friendly?
Decision-making Tools and Techniques
An operational manager will need to make decisions on a daily basis.

This article summarises four commonly used decision making techniques and tools.

Horne, J. (2020) Four Tools and Strategies for Enhanced Decision-Making.


Access the article here: https://www.idashboards.com/4-tools-techniques-for-enhanced-decision-making/

Questions:

Which of the four tools would be most appropriate to use in your operation to make decisions, and why?
Which of the four tools would be inappropriate to use in your operation, and why?
Identify a decision that you need to make and apply one of the tools to this situation. How useful was this tool in helping you to decide on the best way forward?
