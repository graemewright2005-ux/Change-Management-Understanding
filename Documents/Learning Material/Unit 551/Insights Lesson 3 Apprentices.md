The Future of Jobs Report
World Economic Forum (2025). The Future of Jobs Report 2025.

It’s anticipated that technological change will be among several factors to transform the labour market by 2030. This report from the World Economic Forum, an international organisation for public–private company cooperation, examines how these trends may impact jobs and skills, as well as employers’ strategies for working with these changes.


You can access the report here: https://www.weforum.org/publications/the-future-of-jobs-report-2025/digest/

Question: The report highlights that broadening digital access and technologies like AI and automation are expected to have a particular impact on businesses. What simple steps might you take to help a team build their confidence and basic skills in using digital tools in response to this?

Question: Also noted are skills in creative thinking, resilience, flexibility and agility, along with the behaviours of curiosity and lifelong learning. These aspects are expected to increase in importance over the 2025–2030 period. How might you support a team to develop in these areas, as well as focus on lifelong learning?
