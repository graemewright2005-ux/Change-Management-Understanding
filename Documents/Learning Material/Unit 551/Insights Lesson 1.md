Why are Projects Initiated?
This blog summarises seven common reasons why organisations use projects.

Gulec, R. (2014). Why are projects initiated? GreyCampus. 17 Nov.


Access the blog here: https://www.greycampus.com/blog/project-management/why-are-projects-initiated

Questions:

Identify several projects that have been implemented within your organisation, or an organisation that you are familiar with. Which of the reasons proposed within the article does each project relate to?
Did you find it difficult to link some of your projects to one of the reasons proposed? If so, what reason would better explain why the organisation used the project?
In addition to the reasons cited in the blog, other reasons why an organisation might use a project include delivering the organisation’s strategy, achieving competitive advantage, fostering innovation, enabling change and supporting decision-making. Can you identify any projects you are familiar with that relate to any of these reasons?
