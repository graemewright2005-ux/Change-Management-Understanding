UN ‘Sustainable Development Goals’
United Nations (N.D.). The 17 goals. Sustainable Development. Department of Economic and Social Affairs.

This website provides information on the UN’s ‘Sustainable Development Goals’ (SDGs). They form part of the blueprint for achieving “peace and prosperity for people and the planet” as set out in the ‘2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development’.


You can find the goals here: https://sdgs.un.org/goals

Question: Read through the ‘History’ section to see the sequence of events that led to the development of the SDGs. How significant are the SDGs for global sustainability? How important is it that all organisations consider them during their operations?

Question: Under the ‘Implementation Progress’ section, have a look at the progress against targets as detailed in the latest report. For instance, in the 2024 report, Figure 1 (p. 4) shows that only 17% of targets are on track or have been met. However, the same figure is shown for targets that have regressed. What actions do you think would have the greatest impact in keeping the relevant progress figure on track? What role can you and your business play in supporting this?
