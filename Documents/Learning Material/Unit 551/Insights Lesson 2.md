External and Internal Factors to Include in Project Planning
This article summarises the enterprise environmental factors (EEFs) and organisational process assets (OPAs) that need to be considered when planning a project.

Windsor, G. (2021). External and internal factors to include in project planning, 12 Apr.


Access the article here: https://www.brightwork.com/blog/external-and-internal-factors-to-include-in-project-planning

Questions:

Standards are cited as an internal EEF. What organisational standards are likely to impact on the delivery of a project?
Industry and government regulations are likely to impact project planning and delivery. What specific industry regulations would you need to be aware of when managing a project within your organisation?
The article refers to knowledge bases as an example of an OPA. Identify some organisational knowledge bases that you could draw upon when planning how to implement a project.
Ensuring Effective Stakeholder Engagement
This guide offers a number of insights into how organisations can identify key stakeholders and map out a plan to ensure effective engagement in their communications. The guide is aimed at the UK Civil Service, but can be applied to other public sector organisations, as well as those in the private and third sectors.

Government Communication Service (N.D.). Ensuring effective stakeholder engagement.


Access the resource here: https://gcs.civilservice.gov.uk/publications/ensuring-effective-stakeholder-engagement/

Questions:

Section 1, 'What do you want to achieve?', poses a number of key questions to ask before starting any stakeholder activity. How do these questions relate to your own organisation and stakeholders?
Section 3, 'How are you going to engage with them?', outlines how to develop an effective stakeholder engagement plan. Download the template and examine the merits of mapping your stakeholder communication in this way.
